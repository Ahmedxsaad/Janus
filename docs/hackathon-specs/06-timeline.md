## Practical Takeaways

- You have from **July 6** to **August 10, 2026** to register, build, and submit.
- Feedback survey (for the bonus prize) must also be completed **by Aug 10, 2026 (5:00pm ET)**.
- Nothing you build before the Submission Period counts - **Projects must be newly created during the Submission Period** (standard dev tools, libraries, templates, and AI assistants are allowed; other pre-existing code must be disclosed).
- Office hours will be hosted **mid-hackathon** for live Q&A (see `08-resources.md`).

## Submission Modifications

- **Draft Submissions:** Before the Submission Period ends, you may save draft versions to your Devpost portfolio before submitting for evaluation. Once the Submission Period ends, you may **not** change your Submission, but you may keep updating the Project in your Devpost portfolio.
- **Modifications after the Submission Period:** Sponsor/Devpost may permit modifications only to add/remove/replace material that potentially infringes a third-party mark or right, discloses personally identifiable information, or is otherwise inappropriate. The modified Submission must remain substantively the same.
