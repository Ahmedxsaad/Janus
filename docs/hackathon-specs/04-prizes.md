## Prize Administration (from Official Rules)

- **Substitutions & Changes:** Prizes are non-transferable by the winner. Sponsor may substitute a prize of equivalent or greater value at its sole discretion. No prize is awarded if there are no eligible Submissions/Entrants for that prize.
- **Verification Requirement:** Awarding a prize is subject to verification of the identity, qualifications, and role of the potential winner in creating the Submission. No one is a winner until post-competition prize affidavits are completed and verified - even if announced verbally or on the website.
- **Prize Delivery:** Payable to the individual Entrant, the Team's Representative, or the Organization. The Representative allocates a Team/Organization prize among members. Delivered after receipt of completed winner affidavit and other Required Forms. Deadline to return Required Forms: **10 business days** after they are sent. Prizes delivered within **60 days** of Sponsor/Devpost receiving completed Required Forms. Incorrect info may cause delay, disqualification, or forfeiture.
- **Fees & Taxes:** Winners are responsible for any fees (wiring, currency exchange) and for reporting/paying all applicable taxes in their jurisdiction. May need to submit tax forms (US residents: W-9; other countries: W-8BEN) and comply with foreign exchange/banking regulations. Sponsor/Devpost/Prize provider may withhold a portion to comply with tax laws.
